HAESA Grand Community Festival

One Africa, Many Cultures, Unlimited Possibilities.

A yearly gathering that celebrates African talent, culture, innovation and shared humanity — bringing people together across generations, disciplines, languages and cultural backgrounds to build a more peaceful and prosperous Africa.

Vision & mission

A trusted African gathering, with a practical purpose

Vision

To become a trusted African gathering that celebrates diverse cultures, elevates talent, inspires innovation and strengthens peaceful cooperation across communities.

Mission

To bring people together through culture, recognition, learning, dialogue and practical collaboration — creating pathways for African talent and ideas to contribute to thriving communities.

The five festival pillars

What the festival actually celebrates

🏆

Talent Recognition & Empowerment

Celebrating students, researchers, entrepreneurs, artists, educators and emerging problem-solvers.

  • HAESA Excellence Awards, published criteria
  • Live performances & student showcases
  • Mentorship and enterprise networking
🤝

Peace, Harmony & Cross-Cultural Understanding

Helping people encounter one another as individuals and collaborators, not stereotypes.

  • Moderated intercultural dialogue
  • Youth-led peacebuilding activities
  • Clear conduct rules, trained facilitators
🎭

African Cultural Celebration

Celebrating Africa's diversity through living culture, creative expression and respectful exchange.

  • Music, dance, theatre, poetry, storytelling
  • Traditional & contemporary fashion, crafts
  • Food demonstrations & language spaces
⭐

African Icons & Community Heroes

Celebrating historical figures, contemporary achievers and unsung local contributors.

  • African Icons Gallery, sourced biographies
  • Recognition of local changemakers
  • Recorded oral histories, with consent
🌱

African Innovation & Sustainable Living

Showcasing traditional knowledge and modern innovation that improves livelihoods.

  • Green innovation & student exhibitions
  • Food, agriculture, repair & energy ideas
  • Evidence labelled, not overstated

Recognition, done fairly

Awards & selection categories

Student Impact Emerging Entrepreneur Science / Research Contribution Arts & Creative Excellence Cultural Preservation Peacebuilding & Community Service Environmental Innovation Educator / Mentor Recognition African Icon / Lifetime Contribution Unsung Community Hero

Fair selection

  • Criteria, eligibility and deadlines published before nominations open
  • A diverse judging panel; conflicts of interest declared and recused
  • Scored against a published rubric — any public vote carries limited weight

Respect for nominees

  • Respectful notification and a channel for procedural concerns
  • Permission obtained before using names, photos, stories or recordings
  • Awards are never for sale, and sponsorship never buys influence over results
