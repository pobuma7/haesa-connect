We're hiring

Help build Africa's home for History, Archaeology and Economics

HAESA CONNECT is growing, and we're looking for people across Africa to help run it — from partnerships to platform. All roles below are open now.

These are early-stage, remote, part-time roles with room to grow as HAESA CONNECT scales — most will develop into larger or paid positions as the platform and its funding grow. We're a lean, distributed team working across time zones, united by the same mission: make studying History, Archaeology and Economics in Africa feel like joining a continent-wide field, not a solitary path.

Outreach Manager

HybridPart-timeAfrica-wide

Compensation: discussed at interview, based on industry standards and the value you bring.

Lead HAESA CONNECT's relationships with university departments, student societies, cultural institutions and partner organisations across Africa — building the pipeline of partnerships, opportunities and content that make the platform valuable.

Key responsibilities

  • Identify and build relationships with History, Archaeology and Economics departments at African universities
  • Develop partnerships with museums, cultural institutions, NGOs and professional associations
  • Represent HAESA CONNECT at conferences, academic events and student fairs, virtual and in-person
  • Work with the Resource Center and Opportunities teams to source and verify new listings from partners
  • Maintain an outreach pipeline and report on partnership growth

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree, ideally in a related discipline, international relations or communications
  • 2+ years' experience in partnerships, outreach, business development or academic liaison work
  • Strong written and verbal communication in English
  • Working knowledge of the African higher-education landscape
  • Comfortable working independently across time zones

Nice to have

  • French or Portuguese proficiency
  • An existing network within African universities or cultural institutions
  • Experience with CRM tools

Data Analyst

HybridPart-time

Compensation: discussed at interview, based on industry standards and the value you bring.

Turn HAESA CONNECT's growing data — sign-ups, resource engagement, opportunity applications, country-by-country reach — into insight that guides decisions, supports partner reporting, and helps verify the accuracy of published content.

Key responsibilities

  • Build and maintain dashboards tracking growth, engagement and content-verification status
  • Analyse usage patterns to inform which resources, opportunities and languages to prioritise
  • Support fact-verification workflows for scholarships, deadlines and opportunity listings
  • Prepare data summaries for partner reporting and internal decision-making
  • Work with the Media and Technical Director to ensure data quality in the Supabase database

Requirements

  • Proficiency in SQL and comfort working directly with a relational database (PostgreSQL or similar)
  • Experience with a data visualisation tool (Looker Studio, Metabase or similar)
  • Degree or demonstrated experience in Economics, Statistics, Data Science or a related field
  • Ability to communicate findings clearly to a non-technical audience

Nice to have

  • Experience with Supabase specifically
  • Background in nonprofit, edtech or higher-education data
  • French or Portuguese proficiency, for engaging multilingual data
