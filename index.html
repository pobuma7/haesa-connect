What we offer

Three ways HAESA CONNECT works for you

Resources

Capacity development

Guided reading lists, research methods, data and fieldwork skills, and short courses built around what History, Archaeology and Economics graduates actually need on the job.

Community

Connection across institutions

Find classmates, alumni and researchers from departments across the continent. Join discipline-specific groups, ask questions, and get mentorship from people a few years ahead.

Pathways

A guide to global opportunities

A running, curated list of scholarships, fellowships, internships and jobs across business, education, finance, entertainment, export, technology and diplomacy — matched to your discipline.
