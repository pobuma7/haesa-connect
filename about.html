About us

Reawakening the African mind, one discipline at a time

HAESA CONNECT is the community platform of the Historical, Archaeological and Economic Survey Associates (HAESA) — built for students and alumni of History, Archaeology and Economics departments across African institutions.

Vision

To become Africa's leading hub for future-ready university students by bridging classroom learning with real world opportunities while inspiring innovation, leadership, creativity and lifelong learning.

Mission

To equip African university students with knowledge, digital skills, practical resources, mentorship, and opportunities that transform learning into confidence, career readiness, innovation, and meaningful impact for Africa's development.

We started from a simple observation: students of History, Archaeology and Economics across Africa were doing serious, original work — in archives, in the field, in data — largely disconnected from one another and from the opportunities their training should open up.

HAESA CONNECT exists to close that gap. We bring capacity-building resources, a cross-institution community, and a live guide to scholarships, fellowships and jobs into one place, so that studying these disciplines feels like joining a continent-wide field, not a solitary path.

Our members come from universities, museums, research institutes and public and private organisations across all 54 African countries — as current students, recent graduates, and established professionals willing to open a door for the next cohort.

What guides us

Our values

Rigour

We treat History, Archaeology and Economics as serious, employable disciplines, and point members toward resources and opportunities that respect that.

Reach

A member in Accra and a member in Nairobi should be able to find each other as easily as classmates down the hall.

Reciprocity

Alumni open doors for students; students bring fresh research back to the community. The network runs on people giving as much as they take.

Relevance

Opportunities are kept current and sector-specific — business, education, finance, entertainment, export, technology and diplomacy — not a generic list.
